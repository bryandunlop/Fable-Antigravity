myGFO Vision and Roadmap

Five departments share one airplane and one trip at a time. myGFO ties them together where their work overlaps, so every fact is entered once and reaches the right people at the right moment.

The vision

Five circles. Software lives where they overlap.

Each department is a circle and keeps its own tools. Where two circles meet, work changes hands, and that handoff is where myGFO ties them together.

  • The overlaps are today's phone calls.Each one is a handoff. The software turns it into a shared record instead of a conversation nobody wrote down.
  • One record, a view for each side.Both departments work on the same record through their own screen. The Tech Log already shows one aircraft to pilots one way and to maintenance another.
  • Every overlap needs an owner.Someone sets the rules of each handoff, such as the DOM and chief pilot for the Tech Log. Proposed owners are in the reference.

The glidepath: five fixes to the end goal.

Each fix lights up more of the circles. We cross a fix only when its gate is met, not on a date. Today we are at the first fix, landing Document Center and the Tech Log.

What I need

Six decisions keep us on the glidepath.

None of these is a budget request yet. Each one clears a gate that design work alone cannot.

  • Name an owner for each overlap

    Every handoff between two circles needs someone who sets its rules, such as the DOM and chief pilot for the Tech Log.

    Lead team

  • Approve two vendor evaluations

    Jeppesen ForeFlight Airflow for feasibility and briefings, and what aircraft data Gulfstream Performance Analytics can share with us.

    VP

  • Decide who may see live flight times

    Live times for drivers, the protection team and passengers mean some tails are visible on FlightAware. Security should choose which, and for whom.

    Security

  • Discuss capturing actual trip costs

    The SEC disclosure and the entertainment disallowance both need real costs per trip, not estimates.

    Scheduling, VP, CPSO

  • Shape the emergency response app

    It must reach people outside the department without VPN and keep working when everything else is down. P&G Security may already have a platform to build on.

    Security, Safety

  • Sponsor Workday access

    Escalation to managers and joiner and leaver checklists depend on a reliable manager chain from Workday.

    HRIS
