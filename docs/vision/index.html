The end goal

Two apps for crew. Everyone else hears from us where they already are.

The project is done when one real trip runs end to end on production data, with no retyping and no phone call that exists only because a system did not tell someone.

Today and tomorrow

The work gets done today because good people carry it in their heads.

That does not survive a growing fleet or turnover. Here is what changes.

Platform

myGFO connects the systems we already own. It does not replace them.

Eight shared pieces are built once and used by every module. That is how each new feature gets cheaper instead of more tangled.

Principles

Six rules every feature has to follow.

All twelve principles are in the working reference.

Enter once, flow everywhere

A fact has one home. The allergy an EA enters is the allergy the flight attendant reads and the caterer receives.

Tell people only what they need

Alerts come from the state of the world and clear themselves when the condition clears.

Silence is never consent

Missing signatures and overdue items escalate: urgent work to whoever is on call, overdue work to the manager.

Systems of record stay put

CAMP, myairops, Workday and ForeFlight keep their jobs. myGFO connects them.

Machines prepare, humans decide

Automation and AI gather, check, draft and warn. People make the operational calls.

Knowledge compounds

Every trip leaves the department knowing more, through a short debrief reviewed before it becomes guidance.

What we need

Five decisions unlock the next horizons.

None of these is a budget request yet. Each one removes a blocker that design work alone cannot clear.

  • Approve an Airflow evaluation

    See the Jeppesen ForeFlight demos, write the use cases, and bring back a recommendation. Airflow is the bet that keeps crew in two apps.

    VP

  • Decide who may see live flight times

    Live times for drivers, the protection team and passengers mean some tails are visible on FlightAware. Security should choose which, and for whom.

    Security

  • Discuss capturing actual trip costs

    The SEC disclosure and the entertainment disallowance both need real costs per trip, not estimates.

    Scheduling, VP, CPSO

  • Shape the emergency response app

    It must reach people outside the department without VPN, and keep working when everything else is down. P&G Security may already have a platform to build on.

    Security, Safety

  • Sponsor Workday access

    Escalation to managers and joiner and leaver checklists depend on a reliable manager chain from Workday.
