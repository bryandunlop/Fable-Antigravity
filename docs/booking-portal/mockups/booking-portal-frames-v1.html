Frame F

One mechanism, two triggers: watch a route for a seat, or watch dates for the whole fleet freeing up. A watch reserves nothing — when the thing you're watching frees, the EA gets one click into a pre-filled request that flows the normal path.

What to review

  • Watches self-expire after their window passes — no zombie alerts.
  • An opening sends instant email + inbox item (round-2 decision).

Frame I

States & vocabulary — review the words themselves

The lifecycles, spelled out as the chips users will actually see. Wording is design: "Watching" carefully promises nothing; "Freed" says the thing you wanted opened up, not that anything is held.

What to review

  • Trip path keeps Approved and Confirmed separate on purpose.
  • "Fired" is gone — a watch that comes through is now Freed (your word: "if something frees up").
  • Seat asks reuse the trip vocabulary (Requested → Pending → Confirmed) — one language, two paths.

Trip request path

Draft→ Requested→ Pending approval→ Approved→ Confirmed or Declined — reason→ Draft (resubmit)

Watch path

Watching→ Freed→ Requested or Expired

Seat path (Frame G)

Seat requested→ Pending — decision 14:00→ Seat confirmed or Flight changed — reconfirm / Withdrawn / Released

Document states

Valid Flag — < 6 mo after travel Block — expires before travel
