How do threshold alerts surface in the app?

When stockroom water drops below the commissary manager's alert threshold, where does it appear?

A

Notification Bell (Header)

A bell icon in the top nav bar shows a red badge count. Clicking opens a dropdown panel listing unread alerts with timestamp, item name, current qty, and threshold. Alerts can be dismissed or marked read.

B

Dedicated Alerts Page

A full-screen alerts page linked from the commissary manager dashboard and the sidebar nav. Shows alert history, current status per alert, and a "Resolve" action. No header badge — alerts live in one place.

C

Both — Bell + Full Page

Bell in the header for quick awareness (badge count only, no dropdown). Clicking the bell navigates to the full Alerts page. Best of both: always-visible indicator without a cluttered dropdown.
